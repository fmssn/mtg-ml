## Flag 3: missed land drop, no landcycling (model play mistake)

Turn 9, main2, frame 89. No land had been played that turn, and five of the
bot's mana sources were untapped. Options at the flagged decision: `Pass priority`
(0.92, chosen) or `forestcycling {1}` (0.08). The same two

cost of one mana and a card that would otherwise sit in hand. Passing leaves
the land drop unused, so this is a clear mistake: the engine offered the action
and it was legal. The model's value estimate at that point was -0.56.
Confidence: high. Likely cause: cycling abilities are a rarely-rewarded action,
so the model rarely takes it; worth a targeted evaluation case or a look at how
the cycle action is represented (untested hypothesis, no code changed).
